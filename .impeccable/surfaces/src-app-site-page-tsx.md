# Surface brief: PANIVO app (home, doctor, specialty, clinic, settings, admin)

Scope: the working app surfaces. Visitor mode: Operate. Build path: code-led.
Audience and job: patients (any nationality) on mid-range phones in Riyadh find a Saudi specialist, trust the profile, and open WhatsApp to the clinic. The owner manages profiles in /admin.
Constraints: brand blues #0061F3 / #14B4FF / #001A89 on white and light gray, gradients only inside the supplied logos, self-hosted Arabic face, MSA copy, no ratings, no ranking language, no stock doctor photos, server-rendered.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every profile carries a verification stamp: a round ink-blue seal holding the consent and last-checked date, pressed next to the facts it vouches for. It refuses the category default of a white list of interchangeable doctor cards with star ratings and stock portraits.

OWN-WORLD: White slips on a light-gray counter. One sturdy Arabic sans (Readex Pro) at four sizes. #0061F3 owns whole regions (the search field band, active states); #001A89 is stamp ink and the single reversed plate per screen; #14B4FF only for selection and focus. Cards are slips with a dashed tear line between the facts and the contact stub. The stamp is the only ornament.

STORY: The visitor sees that every doctor here was checked, believes it because the date and source sit beside each fact, and taps WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar: wordmark (start), favourites (end). Below, a full-width #0061F3 band holding one large white search field and five specialty tiles. Then the first shelf title and the start of large swipeable slips (about 1.2 visible), each with name, role, clinic, district, stamp, and a WhatsApp stub. Floating tab bar above the bottom edge.

FORM: Appointment slip with a verification stamp, grounded candidate 4 of 7. Signature move: the stamp presses onto a slip when it enters view (scale 1.12 to 1, ink settles), static under reduced motion. seed:06449bd6aa703122
